Welcome to the blog

Jun 2026 · 2 min read
meta

I've wanted a place to write for a while — somewhere that isn't a Slack thread or a half-finished Notion page. So here it is. Nothing fancy, just notes worth keeping.

Mostly two kinds of posts, and they'll probably stay separate enough that you can pick whichever you came for:

  • Engineering — distributed systems, Rust, latency, and the occasional postmortem-shaped story from working on CloudFront's data plane.
  • Camping — trip reports, gear notes, and routes from Ireland's coastline and beyond. Tent, fire, no signal.

Every now and then there might be something else entirely — competitive programming, learning Korean, or whatever I'm currently obsessing over. The blog index has tag filters if you only care about one thing.

No grand reason. I write things down anyway — might as well do it somewhere others can read, and somewhere I can look back on later. If something here is useful or you just want to talk about any of it, say hello.
