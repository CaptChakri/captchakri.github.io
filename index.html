Who I Am

I've loved problem-solving for as long as I can remember — the more tangled the problem, the more I want to sit with it until it gives up a clean, simple answer. That's the thread running through everything I do.

It's what pulls me toward distributed systems: by day I keep large-scale internet infrastructure fast and reliable when it would much rather fall over. The roles and the detail are in the descent log just below.

Competitive programming scratches the same itch — a hard puzzle with an elegant solution. I'm a LeetCode Knight (1800+), with a few national robotics and coding finishes along the way.

And when I'm not at a keyboard, I'm outside — tent, fire, no signal. Best debugging happens away from the screen anyway. You'll meet that campfire further down.
