// a bit about me自己紹介 · 자기소개

I grew up in India, studied computer science, and found myself completely hooked by the problem of making distributed systems not fail — or at least, fail gracefully.

At AWS I work on CloudFront's data plane — the edge infrastructure that routes requests, caches content, and keeps things humming. I've shipped work that shaved 50–75% off connection latency, cut inter-region round trips by 60%, and built fault-isolated control-plane services that shrink blast radius when things go wrong.

Outside of work I like competitive programming — there's something satisfying about a hard algorithmic problem with a clean solution. I'm a LeetCode Knight (1800+) and I've competed in national robotics and coding competitions.

When I'm not staring at terminals, you'll find me camping — tent, fire, no signal. Ireland's west coast is full of spots where the only latency that matters is how long the kettle takes. Best debugging happens away from the keyboard anyway.

If you're working on something interesting in infrastructure, distributed systems, or just want to chat — I'd love to hear from you.
