Counting-Sheep Playground

// playground

Counting sheep.

A bedtime toy that quietly cheats in your favour — vivid imagery and slow breathing instead of a bare number, because that's what the research says actually works.

Watch the flock amble in under a dim night sky. Tap Breathe for a 4–2–6 rhythm and Lullaby for a soft Korean cradle song. The longer you watch, the darker it gets. Then go to bed.
